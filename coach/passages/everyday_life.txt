# Everyday life — a weekend
At the weekend, I don't have to get up as early as I do during the week, so I usually stay in bed until about eight o'clock. Once I've got up, I open the windows and make myself a big breakfast. I'll often have eggs, toast, and a cup of coffee while I'm listening to music.

After breakfast, I clean up the kitchen and decide what I'm going to do. If the weather's nice, I'll go for a walk in the park. There are usually lots of people there, and I sometimes see neighbors I haven't spoken to for a while. We'll stop and have a chat about what we've been doing and what we're planning to do later.

When I get home, I might do some housework. I'll put some clothes in the washing machine, tidy up my room, and take out the rubbish. I don't particularly enjoy cleaning, but I know I'll feel better when everything's neat and tidy. Once I've finished, I'll make some lunch and sit down for a while.

In the afternoon, I often meet up with a friend. We might go shopping, have a coffee, or just walk around town. Sometimes we'll find a quiet place to sit and talk. It's nice to have time to catch up without having to rush off somewhere.

In the evening, I usually stay at home. I'll cook something simple, and then I'll watch a film or a few episodes of a series. If I'm not too tired, I'll call my family and see how they're getting on. We'll talk about everyday things, but it's always good to hear their voices.

Before I go to bed, I check what I've got planned for the next day. I might prepare my clothes, charge my phone, and set my alarm. Although weekends go by quickly, I try to make the most of them. I don't need to do anything extraordinary. Sometimes it's enough to have a good meal, spend time with people I care about, and enjoy a bit of peace and quiet.
